What Lingroo does not claim

Lingroo is an independent learning product. Its six Exam Mode packs are original exam-oriented practice, not official model tests from telc, Goethe-Institut or TestDaF. Speaking practice is still in development.

Frequently asked questions

What can I use for free?

You can explore Lingroo and use a limited part of the C1 library, including 5 listening items, 5 reading texts, 1 C1 writing topic, 200 C1 vocabulary entries and 100 C1 synonym tasks. Access follows the current app logic.

What is included in the 14-day tester account?

After registration, tester access works like Premium for 14 days, so you can assess all main modules before subscribing.

Is B2 included?

Yes. Lingroo includes B2 and C1 vocabulary, synonyms, gap tasks, reading, listening, writing and three original Exam Mode packs for each level.

Is Lingroo an official exam simulation?

No. Lingroo complements official model tests but does not replace them. The tasks are independently created and train skills relevant to B2/C1.

Is speaking practice available?

Not yet as a finished core module. It is in development and is not sold as an already available Premium feature.
