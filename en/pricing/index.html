What Lingroo does not claim

Lingroo is an independent learning tool. The six training sets are original materials, not official papers from telc, Goethe-Institut or TestDaF. Speaking practice is still in development.

Frequently asked questions

What can I use for free?

You can explore Lingroo and use a limited part of the C1 library: 5 listening materials, 5 reading texts, 1 writing topic, 200 C1 vocabulary entries and 100 synonym tasks. B2 is much more open during the current test phase.

What does the 14-day full trial include?

After registration, you receive the same access as Premium for 14 days and can test all main areas.

Does the price include B2 and C1?

Yes. Premium includes the currently available content at both levels.

Is this an official exam simulation?

No. Lingroo complements official model tests but does not replace them.

Is speaking practice available?

Not yet as a finished module. It is in development.
