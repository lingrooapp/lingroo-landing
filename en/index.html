What is Lingroo?

Lingroo is a web app for German at B2 and C1. It includes short exercises on vocabulary, language structures, writing, reading and listening.

Who is Lingroo for?

For learners who are around B2, preparing for C1, or working gradually from B2 towards C1.

How long is one study session?

Many exercises take 5 to 10 minutes. Exam mode also offers longer sets.

What is included in the 14-day access?

For 14 days, you can use every currently available B2 and C1 section, including writing guidance, exam mode and saved progress.

How much does full access cost?

After the 14 days, it costs €4.99 per month and can be cancelled monthly.

Can I use Lingroo without a paid plan?

Yes. Selected exercises remain free. The exact access details are listed on the pricing page.

Is Lingroo an official exam simulator?

No. Lingroo is an independent learning tool designed to complement official sample papers, courses and books.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Lingroo runs in the browser and can be added to your home screen as a web app.

Which languages are supported?

The app interface is in German. Vocabulary translations are available in Polish, Ukrainian, Russian and English.

Is speaking practice available?

Not yet. Short speaking tasks are planned; the current focus is vocabulary, language structures, writing, reading and listening.
