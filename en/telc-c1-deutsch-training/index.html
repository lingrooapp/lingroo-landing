Key questions before you start

What can I try for free?

You can solve interactive sample tasks, see feedback and decide whether this learning style fits you. For regular practice, saved progress and the full training flow, the CTA opens the Lingroo app.

Does Lingroo replace official telc or Goethe materials?

No. Official mock exams and practice materials are still important. Lingroo complements them with short interactive tasks, feedback, repetition and progress tracking for grammar gaps, writing, vocabulary, reading and listening.

Who is this page for?

For B2/C1 learners who are not starting from zero and want targeted exam-style practice: verb-preposition patterns, connectors, fixed structures, text logic and formal writing.

What happens after I click “keep practicing”?

You open the Lingroo app. There you can solve more tasks, save your progress and later decide whether Premium access is useful for your regular preparation.

Is Lingroo officially affiliated with telc, Goethe or TestDaF?

No. Lingroo is an independent learning product. Exam names are used only for orientation, so learners understand what type of preparation the tasks are designed for.
